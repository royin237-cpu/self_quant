Strategy Summary: Small Cap Low Price Weekly Rotation with IPO Filter and Performance Metrics

Key Features:
1. Stock Selection:
   - Universe: Shanghai and Shenzhen main board stocks (codes starting with sh.60 or sz.00)
   - Weekly rebalancing: every Wednesday (last trading day of the week)
   - IPO filter: Only stocks listed for more than 1 year (using column 30 as IPO date)
   - Fundamental filters:
        * Non-stock status (tradestatus == 1)
        * Non-ST stocks (isST == 0)
        * Positive peTTM and pbMRQ
   - Selection logic:
        * From valid pool, take the 50 smallest by market value (流通市值)
        * From these 50, select the 10 lowest priced stocks (close price)

2. Trading Rules:
   - Buy at closing price (no slippage assumed in this version)
   - Sell at closing price (no slippage assumed in this version)
   - Transaction fees:
        * Commission: max(5 CNY, 0.025% of transaction value)
        * Transfer fee: 0.001% of transaction value
        * Stamp duty: 0.05% of transaction value (only on sell)
   - Position limits: Maximum 10 stocks
   - Handling of special cases:
        * Delisted stocks: Position value marked to zero (full loss)
        * Suspended stocks: Position held, no trade executed
        * Limit up/down: No trade executed, position held

3. Performance Metrics Calculated:
   - Total return
   - Annualized return
   - Maximum drawdown (percentage)
   - Maximum drawdown duration (in days)
   - Sharpe ratio (using 3% annual risk-free rate)
   - Annual returns breakdown

4. Outputs:
   - Excel file with detailed trade log: 小市值低价轮动策略明细_每周_含持仓10只_清退市_增强版.xlsx
   - Plots:
        * Equity curve (strategy total assets over time)
        * Drawdown chart (showing underwater periods)
